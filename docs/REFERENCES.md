   Electron Microscopy*. NIST, 2017. https://www.nist.gov/publications/dimensional-measurement-nanostructures-scanning-electron-microscopy
2. NIST Special Publication 1170, *Documentation for Reference Material RM 8820*.
   https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.1170
3. JCGM 100:2008, *Evaluation of measurement data — Guide to the expression of
   uncertainty in measurement*. https://www.bipm.org/en/committees/jc/jcgm/publications
4. Modarres, M. H. et al. *Neural network for nanoscience scanning electron
   microscope image recognition*. Scientific Data 5, 180172 (2018).
   https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2018.172
5. Citation File Format specification. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4813122

These references inform good practice and validation context. They do not imply
that the workbench is NIST-certified or standards-compliant by itself.
