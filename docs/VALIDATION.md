# Validation and trial data

## What is validated

The automated suite checks calibration geometry, common scale-bar styles,
circle/rectangle/grating/particle models, periodicity, roughness descriptors,
cross-section datums, layer review, waveguide width/path, line-profile chords,
population classes, contact QA and process-study calculations.

Synthetic images generated by `examples/generate_validation_samples.py` have
known geometry and are BSD-3-Clause project material. They are suitable for
learning the interface and regression trials, not for estimating real SEM
uncertainty or edge bias.

## External trial sources

External images are not copied into this repository. Users may separately obtain
appropriately licensed datasets and retain their original attribution/license:

- **ImageDataResource SEM dataset** accompanying Modarres et al., *Scientific
  Data* 2018, an openly available human-annotated nanoscience SEM dataset under
  CC BY 4.0: https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2018.172
- **UOC Multi-magnification SEM Image Dataset**, Mendeley Data, CC BY 4.0:
  https://doi.org/10.17632/74k85vybmk.1
- **NIST Detection Limits for SEM Image Segmentation**, simulated SEM image
  collections and metrics under the NIST open-data terms:
  https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/detection-limits-for-sem-image-segmentation-fc250

Review each dataset's intended task. Most public SEM datasets do not provide a
traceable dimensional ground truth and therefore test robustness/usability, not
absolute dimensional accuracy.

## Laboratory validation before publication or production

1. Verify magnification/scale using a suitable calibrated reference material.
2. Establish repeatability across fields, sessions and operators.
3. Compare against an independent/reference method where appropriate.
4. Quantify sampling, calibration, edge-model, image-quality and model-selection
   contributions to uncertainty.
5. Lock a versioned recipe and acceptance criteria.
6. Retain raw images, overlays, exclusions and audit/export files.
