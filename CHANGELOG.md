# Changelog

- Native standalone Windows metrology workbench.
- Eight physical measurement recipes plus line gauge and signal-texture tools.
- Operator-correctable calibration and traceable QA states.
- Class-aware and contact-aware statistics and interactive distributions.
- Cross-section populations, layer-interface review and process/dose studies.
- CSV, PNG and selectable professional PDF reporting.
- Bundled tutorial, troubleshooting, citation, privacy and validation material.
